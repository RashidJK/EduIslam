### Text handling rules

The app never types, generates, or edits Quranic text. Exactly two display rules apply, both
implemented in `src/domain/quran/text.ts` and tested against real provider data
(`tests/fixtures/quran-first-verses.json`):

1. **Byte-order mark.** The provider's first verse begins with `U+FEFF`, an encoding artifact. It
   is removed. Nothing else is stripped.
2. **Bismillah.** In every surah except Al-Fatiha (1) and At-Tawbah (9), the provider prefixes the
   Bismillah to verse 1. The app shows that prefix — exactly as supplied — as an un-numbered
   heading above verse 1. Detection compares the letters (ignoring marks) of the first four words;
   this handles the two spellings in the data (surahs 95 and 97 use a shadda variant). The split is
   verified to be lossless: `heading + " " + verse 1 === original` for all 112 surahs.

Diacritics and orthography are otherwise untouched. Search removes diacritics **for matching
only**.

### Integrity checks (every surah, on arrival)

`src/domain/quran/integrity.ts` refuses to render a surah unless the verse count matches the
canonical metadata, verse numbers run 1..n, every verse's global number is correct, and no verse
is empty. Failures show a "couldn't be verified" message — partial text is never shown.

Surah names and verse counts are generated by `npm run quran:metadata` from the provider and
checked against canonical totals (114 surahs, 6,236 verses).

## Hijri dates

Calculated on the device with ICU calendars (`Intl.DateTimeFormat`): Umm al-Qura (default),
tabular civil, tabular astronomical-epoch, and an astronomical estimate. The method and any local
day adjustment are always shown, and every date is labelled as an estimate that may differ by a
day or two with moon sighting. If the device cannot compute the chosen calendar, the app says so
rather than showing a Gregorian date.

Occasion **date rules** (e.g. Eid al-Fitr on 1 Shawwal) are bundled as calendar structure in
`src/content/library.ts` and marked for reviewer confirmation. Occasion **learning content** is
unpublished until written and reviewed.

## Bundled content

Only non-religious material ships in the app bundle: two app-guide lessons and three Arabic-letter
recognition lessons, labelled as such. Duas, Salah guides, stories, and religious courses are
published only through the CMS after review (see `CONTENT_REVIEW.md`).
